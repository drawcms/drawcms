# Security Policy

## Supported versions

Until the first stable release, security fixes are applied to the latest
commit on the default branch and to the most recent published package.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Do not open a public issue. Email `dimasna96@gmail.com` with:

- the affected version or commit;
- reproduction steps or a proof of concept;
- the likely impact; and
- any suggested mitigation.

Do not access other users' data, degrade a live service, or publish the issue
before a coordinated disclosure date is agreed.

The maintainer aims to acknowledge a report within three business days and
provide an initial assessment within seven business days. These targets are
not a guarantee.
